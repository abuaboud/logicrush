# LogicRush

Arabic-language (RTL) platform for logic problems and timed contests. This repo
is the 2026 rebuild of logicrush.com, which ran on Spring Boot + Angular from
2018.

## Areas

- [Engineering](./engineering/index.md) — stack conventions, the Craftspace
  house style, the legacy system we are replacing.
- [Decisions](./decisions/) — hard-to-reverse calls and their why.
- [MEMORY](./MEMORY.md) — dated one-off notes not yet worth their own page.
